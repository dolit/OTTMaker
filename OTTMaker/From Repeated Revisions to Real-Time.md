## **Challenges of Traditional Interface Editing** 

Under traditional development workflows, interface adjustments often requires coordination across multiple teams and processes. This not only increases communication costs but also extends project timelines. Especially during major sporting events, content launches, or other activities, traditional cycles can cause operators to miss valuable traffic opportunities. 

In addition, traditional editing systems separate the editing area from the preview environment. Operators can’t instantly see the changes on the interface. If the outcome has problems, staff must repeat the cycle of modification, deployment, and verification, resulting in significant time loss and increasing costs. 

For modern OTT/IPTV platforms in a rapidly changing market, this workflow is no longer efficient enough. 

## **WYSIWYG: Making Interface Editing Intuitive and Efficient** 

To address these operational challenges, **WYSIWYG (What You See Is What You Get)** interface editing has emerged as the ideal solution. 

This approach combines the editing environment and the front-end display into a unified workspace. As operators make adjustments in the back-end, they can **immediately see** the final interface.Every modification is reflected on the preview screen in real time, eliminating the need to constantly switch between pages for verification. 

The greatest value of WYSIWYG is the combination of editing and preview. It puts an end to inefficient repetitive workflows and fundamentally solves the problems of endless revisions and low productivity. Teams can identify issues instantly, make adjustments immediately, and confirm the final result before deployment.
